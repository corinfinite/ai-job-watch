About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

As a Manager of Beneficial Deployments Customer Success at Anthropic, you'll be a pivotal front-line leader managing Customer Success Managers who drive value across our portfolio of Beneficial Deployments Customers. Anthropic’s Beneficial Deployments team is focused on building AI for good through partnerships with high-impact organizations across education, public health, life sciences, economic mobility, and more. You'll directly coach and develop a team of 4+ CSMs while maintaining strategic oversight of high-impact customer engagements. This role demands exceptional people leadership combined with deep product knowledge and customer success acumen to guide your team in maximizing customer value and expanding our footprint across mission-driven organizations. This will be a player-coach role: in addition to leading the team, you will directly own a few strategic accounts, keeping you close to the day-to-day work of driving partner impact and letting you model excellence for your team . You'll be instrumental in building scalable CS processes, methodologies, and playbooks that will define how we grow and mature as an organization.

Responsibilities:

  • Directly manage and develop a team of 4-8 Customer Success Managers supporting strategic nonprofit accounts 

  • Serve as a player-coach by directly owning a few strategic accounts, driving outcomes for our highest-impact partnerships while demonstrating best-in-class customer success practices for the team.

  • Join strategic customer engagements and escalations, demonstrating executive presence with C-level stakeholders across flagship partnerships 

  • Help shape our systems integrator and implementation partner model, partnering internally to extend delivery capacity and reach far more nonprofits than we can serve one to one

  • Serve as a visible voice on AI adoption in the nonprofit and mission-driven sector, represent Anthropic at sector convenings, customer-facing forums, and bring that external point of view back to sharpen our strategy

  • Build and refine scalable CS processes including success planning frameworks, QBR templates, and repeatable sector playbooks

  • Partner closely with Sales to identify expansion opportunities, facilitate smooth handoffs, and ensure aligned account strategies

  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Partnerships, Marketing, and Policy to advocate for customer needs and drive a seamless customer journey

  • Contribute to hiring, onboarding, and training initiatives as the Customer Success organization scales

  • Establish and nurture an AI-first, innovation-focused team culture, improving team processes and deepening team hands-on expertise

  • Conduct regular 1:1s, performance reviews, and career development planning for direct reports

  • Serve as escalation point for at-risk accounts and complex customer situations within your team's portfolio

  • Develop customer advocacy programs including case studies, testimonials, and communities of practice

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
