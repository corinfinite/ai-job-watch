About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

Program Overview

Many of the questions that will shape our future have no single checkable answer. Reasoning well about them, whether in law, policy, philosophy, economics, cognitive science, or AI safety itself, takes the kind of judgment experts spend years building. We believe frontier AI, developed safely, can become a real partner on these questions, but getting there means working closely with the people who know these fields best.

This fellowship brings researchers and experts from philosophy, cognitive science, economics, policy, and law into Anthropic for four months to work alongside our research teams. Fellows pair deep domain expertise with our frontier models and internal tooling to evaluate, improve, and apply Claude's capabilities in their field.

What to Expect:

  • A concrete, scoped project matched to your background, with mentorship from Anthropic researchers.
  • Access to frontier Claude models and internal evaluation tooling.
  • A cohort of incoming Fellows to collaborate with and a connection to Anthropic's broader research community.

Compensation: 

$100,000–$167,000 USD for the four-month fellowship (equivalent to $300,000–$500,000 USD annualized), and other benefits.

Key responsibilities

  • Identifying key open problems in your field, assessing approaches to solving them, and using Claude to make progress on them.
  • Identifying grand societal challenges which the expertise of your field is crucial for addressing, and assessing Claude’s strengths and weaknesses in tackling them.
  • Designing evaluations and RL environments that test models in your field systematically, turning your expertise into working tests of how well Claude reasons in your field.
  • Team up with other Fellows in your field to build environments that push Claude on the hardest, most judgment-heavy problems your discipline has to offer.

Minimum qualifications

  • Are excited about the potential for AI to reason well about hard questions in their field where expert judgment matters.
  • Hands-on experience using Claude and Claude Code in your day-to-day workflow.
  • Hold (or are completing) a PhD in philosophy, cognitive science, economics, policy or law, or have equivalent research experience.
  • Are comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-agency atmosphere for a few months and delivering concrete results.
  • Existing authorization to work in the United States for the duration of the program.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience designing evaluations, benchmarks, datasets, or expert rubrics.
  • Demonstrated expertise in your field, shown through research, scholarship, or expert-level analytical writing.

Representative projects

  • A philosopher building hard cases that require normative judgment in ethics or formal epistemology.
  • A cognitive scientist designing experimental-design and model-comparison tasks.
  • An economist writing modelling and identification problems with ground truth.
  • A policy scholar drafting regulatory and cost-benefit analysis tasks with expert rubrics.
  • A legal scholar building AI-policy-relevant law datasets.

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
