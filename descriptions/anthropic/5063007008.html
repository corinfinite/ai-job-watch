About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the Role

We're looking for a Software Engineer to join the team building Claude Security. In this role, you'll take new security capabilities in frontier models and turn them into products that customers can rely on, working across the full stack from early prototype through production.

This role sits close to both research and go-to-market. You'll work with researchers on the team to understand what the models can do and where they fall short, and with customers and partners to understand which problems are worth solving for them. You'll be expected to form your own view from both and to own significant pieces of the roadmap, including deciding what not to pursue.

The right candidate is a strong generalist engineer with the technical depth to engage with research, the product instincts to know what's worth building, and the drive to move fast. Prior security experience is useful but not required; range across the stack and good judgment about what to build matter more.

Responsibilities

  • Build and ship user-facing and/or backend features for Claude Security
  • Build the agent loops, tool integrations, and enabling infrastructure for cybersecurity products
  • Collaborate tightly with product managers and go-to-market to understand user needs, shape feature scope, and make informed tradeoffs as you build
  • Iterate quickly based on user feedback and internal testing, improving the experience on a continuous basis
  • Collaborate with research teams to identify and develop new model capabilities for security applications

You may be a good fit if you:

  • Contribute across the stack, from front-end UI to back-end infrastructure
  • Are proficient in TypeScript, Python, and/or Go and have experience building distributed systems
  • Enjoy fast iteration and are energized by prototyping new ideas
  • Are comfortable working closely with research and go-to-market teams
  • Have strong communication skills and can work effectively across functions
  • Have 7+ years of experience as a software engineer

Strong candidates may also have:

  • Experience building client-side terminal applications
  • Experience developing cybersecurity products
  • Experience in incident response, reverse engineering, network analysis, penetration testing, or similar fields
  • Experience building skills, plugins, or hooks in Claude Code or similar tools
  • Experience building with Claude Managed Agents or similar frameworks

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
