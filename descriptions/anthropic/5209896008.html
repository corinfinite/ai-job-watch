About the role

We are seeking a Strategic Finance partner to join our Finance team at Anthropic, focused on operating expenses (OpEx) with a primary partnership across our Engineering organization. In this role, you will be the financial partner to engineering leaders — helping them make smart, well-informed decisions about headcount, vendor spend, tooling, and the operational investments that fuel our research and product work. As we continue to grow rapidly, you will play a crucial role in shaping how we plan, allocate, and steward OpEx across the company.

You'll build driver-based models that tie vendor and tooling spend to the underlying technical inputs (usage, seats, throughput, capacity), and you'll be expected to engage substantively with engineering leaders on the technical realities behind those drivers — and to push back with conviction when the numbers don't add up. The strongest partners on this team aren't order-takers; they ask sharp questions, challenge assumptions, and help engineering leaders land on better decisions than they'd reach alone. If you're excited about getting close to the technology, holding your ground in a debate with a thoughtful engineering leader, and helping a fast-growing AI company invest its resources well, we'd love to hear from you.

Key responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary finance partner to Engineering leaders, providing guidance and recommendations on vendor and tooling spend, and resource allocation decisions.

  • Build and maintain driver-based financial models for engineering OpEx — tying vendor and tooling spend to the underlying technical drivers (usage, seats, throughput, capacity, utilization) rather than top-down growth assumptions

  • Develop a deep understanding of the engineering tooling and vendor stack so you can engage substantively with technical leaders on what's driving spend and where it's headed

  • Lead budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning for the engineering organization, partnering with leaders to translate roadmaps into resource plans

  • Push back constructively on assumptions, proposals, and forecasts — bringing data, sharp questions, and clear reasoning to help engineering leaders land on better decisions

  • Prepare clear, concise financial reports, analyses, and variance commentary that highlight key trends, risks, and opportunities for engineering and finance stakeholders

  • Establish reporting dashboards and scorecards that track engineering headcount, vendor spend efficiency, and other key operational metrics

  • Drive analyses on build-vs-buy decisions, vendor consolidation, contract negotiations, and organizational design tradeoffs where finance can sharpen engineering decision-making

  • Partner cross-functionally with Strategic Finance, FP&A, Accounting, Recruiting, and People teams to ensure engineering plans are aligned with broader financial strategy

  • Participate in resource allocation exercises to ensure clarity around headcount prioritization

Minimum qualifications

  • Have experience in FP&A, OpEx or G&A finance preferably at high-growth technology companies

  • Have demonstrated experience building driver-based financial models — tying spend to operational and technical inputs rather than top-down growth rates

  • Are comfortable with technical concepts and have a genuine interest in understanding the systems, tools, and infrastructure behind the line items you're modeling

  • Have a track record of pushing back constructively on senior stakeholders — bringing data and clear reasoning to disagreements rather than deferring to the loudest voice in the room

  • Demonstrate excellent communication and storytelling abilities, with experience presenting to C-suite executives and board members

  • Have a bias toward action, strong work ethic, and experience driving operational outcomes under tight timelines

  • Thrive in fast-paced, dynamic environments and can navigate ambiguity with confidence

  • Possess strong collaboration skills and can build effective partnerships across functions and levels

  • Are passionate about Anthropic's mission to build safe, beneficial AI systems

Preferred qualifications

  • Have experience at a high-growth technology company or startup

  • Possess expertise with modern financial planning tools (e.g., Anaplan, Pigment)

  • Have direct experience partnering with Engineering, R&D, or other technical organizations as a finance business partner

  • Have hands-on experience modeling vendor spend with technical drivers — cloud/SaaS usage, seat-based tooling, observability and data infrastructure costs, etc.

  • Possess a technical background or coursework (CS, engineering, quantitative field) or comparable self-taught fluency

  • Have experience leading headcount planning and workforce modeling at scale

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
