About the role

Finance systems are critical to how Anthropic operates and scales. As the Finance Systems Production Support Lead, you will set the strategy for how we support those systems, bringing operational rigor and one consistent support model across the finance portfolio.

You will lead a lean support team with US and international coverage, set the service levels Finance can rely on, and make sure each incident leaves the systems stronger than it found them.

This is also a chance to redefine what production support looks like, using Claude to scale and transform how we support finance systems. Claude will handle more of the routine work over time, and you will own the exceptions, the quality bar and the patterns that feed back into the product roadmap. This team will cover our core platforms, including homegrown systems and applications built on Claude.

Responsibilities

  • Own the support model: Build one operating model for tickets, access and incidents across finance applications, including ERP, close, procure to pay, revenue, planning, tax and treasury systems, plus new internally built applications.
  • Lead a lean support team: Manage the support team day to day, including capacity, performance and escalation paths. You will also be the team's backup, working tickets and incidents yourself as needed.
  • Design the rotation and operating rigor: Design the US and international support rotation, including close-week coverage and handoffs. Set the routines that keep support consistent, such as triage, runbooks, postmortems and regular service reviews.
  • Drive operational excellence: Shape lean, scalable support processes that grow with the business, and implement leading practices across how the team works.
  • Build training and onboarding: Create the training and onboarding that gets new support team members productive quickly and prepares Finance users for new systems and releases.
  • Set and meet service levels: Define SLAs by priority and category, run triage, and plan coverage around month-end and quarter-end close.
  • Run incidents and root cause analysis: Lead sensitive and time-critical production issues from first response to communication with Finance leaders, then drive root causes to a fix so they don't recur.
  • Own release management: Run release testing and deployment across the portfolio, including Workday's twice-yearly releases. Partner with Product Owners on what to test, and put test automation ahead of added headcount.
  • Run support by the metrics: Report ticket volume, time to resolve, SLA attainment, defects that escape a release, and automation coverage. Use the data to drive decisions, and turn the trends into input for each product roadmap.
  • Make support audit-ready: Work with Product Owners and Internal Audit so tickets, access changes, support-run changes and runbooks hold up to SOX and public company expectations.
  • Run recurring user access reviews: Own the recurring user access review cycle across finance applications, with evidence that stands up to audit.

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience running production support for enterprise finance applications, ideally including Workday Financials or a comparable ERP, in a live, high-volume environment
  • Experience leading a lean support function, including coverage across time zones
  • Experience shaping lean, scalable support processes using leading practices, including SLAs, triage, incident management, root cause analysis and runbooks, and running them with metrics that leaders actually use
  • Knowledge of how finance works day to day, including close, procure to pay
  • Understanding of SOX controls for change management and user access, including recurring access reviews, and the ability to produce evidence that stands up to audit
  • Experience leading release management, including testing and deployment, with a practical view on what to automate first
  • Ability to stay calm and clear under pressure, and to explain an incident and its risk to both accountants and engineers
  • Hands-on skills: reading an integration error, querying data with SQL and digging into a ticket yourself when that's what it takes
  • Excitement about using Claude and other AI tools to scale and transform how support is done, with good judgment about where a human must stay in the loop

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with Jira Service Management, including workflows, queues and dashboards
  • Experience supporting integrations across billing, procurement, expense, tax, planning or treasury platforms
  • Experience with internally built finance applications or data pipelines, such as BigQuery, dbt or Airflow
  • Experience scaling finance systems to public-company standards
  • Workday certifications, or an ITIL or similar service management credential
  • Experience building AI agents or automation for ticket handling or testing

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
