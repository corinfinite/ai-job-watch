About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

As an Enterprise Account Executive, you'll own Anthropic's relationships with hedge funds, market makers, and proprietary trading firms across EMEA. These are among our most advanced customers: their engineers, researchers, and traders use Claude every day, test every claim, and set a very high bar for performance, security, and protection of their intellectual property.

You'll take firms from Claude Code in engineering into quantitative research, trading, risk, and operations, and then firm-wide, shaping multi-year partnerships with founders and technology leaders. You'll work closely with Applied AI architects, and bring what these customers need back to our product and research teams. The role is based in London, with regular travel to Amsterdam and other European cities.

Key responsibilities

  • Own a book of strategic, high-value accounts across systematic, multi-strategy, and macro hedge funds, market makers, and proprietary trading firms, building and executing account plans that prioritize the highest-potential opportunities, and carrying and exceeding an individual quota
  • Engage personally with C-level, research, and senior technical stakeholders (founders, Chief Investment Officers, Chief Operating Officers, CTOs, CISOs, Heads of Quantitative Research, Heads of Engineering, Heads of Data/AI), building business cases and value narratives
  • Lead accounts through technical evaluation and procurement, security, data governance, regulatory, and legal review, through to production deployment and expansion
  • Win new firms and grow existing ones
  • Win head-to-head evaluations against other model providers and in-house builds
  • Structure and negotiate multi-year, usage-based commitments, then drive the adoption that delivers them
  • Expand adoption of Claude from engineering teams to quantitative researchers, data scientists, portfolio managers, traders, risk, and operations, including in firms where teams operate independently and keep their work separate
  • Run a disciplined pipeline and forecasting cadence, with accurate deal inspection, clear next steps, and predictable execution across a high-value, usage-driven book of business
  • Partner closely with Applied AI, Solutions Architecture, and Product to design solutions, support rigorous customer evaluations, prove value quickly, and translate the needs of hedge funds and trading firms (research, engineering, data, risk, operations) into product and roadmap input
  • Codify winning use cases, proof points, and reference stories from your deals while respecting customer confidentiality, partnering with Marketing and Enablement to help scale them across the broader financial services motion
  • Work with US colleagues on global firms, and with our banking team
  • Orchestrate cross-functional and partner motions with Customer Success, Legal, Partnerships, and cloud partners to deliver a seamless customer experience, and represent Anthropic as a visible, trusted voice with customers and at industry events

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
