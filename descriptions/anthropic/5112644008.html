About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

Anthropic seeks a strategic communications professional to lead our communications programs and brand expansion across Europe, with an initial focus on the UK, France, and Germany. You will drive communications across product, corporate, and policy, with a particular emphasis on enterprise communications in the region. Working closely with European spokespeople and cross-functional partners in GTM, Applied AI, policy, marketing, and more, you will shape how Anthropic shows up across all key audiences in the market. You will report to our Head of EMEA Communications.

The ideal candidate brings deep expertise in the European AI landscape—knowing the key players in the business, political, and academic worlds who are shaping the conversation about AI safety. We're looking for someone who can flex beyond traditional PR to work with influencers and drive content marketing opportunities with enterprise partners. 

Responsibilities

  • Shape and drive the European communications strategy, helping us bring Anthropic's global momentum, leadership vision, product innovation, and human touch to European audiences
  • Lead enterprise communications across Europe, partnering with spokespeople and major customers on media interviews, speaking opportunities, bylines, and social content
  • Identify and develop content marketing opportunities with major European enterprise customers, surfacing compelling customer stories and co-marketing initiatives
  • Manage communications around European GTM events, working closely with our spokespeople to prepare them for firesides, keynotes, and other speaking engagements. 
  • Build relationships with journalists, analysts, and influencers across European markets—with particular depth in AI and technology coverage
  • Shape social media strategy for our GTM leaders, with a focus on LinkedIn
  • Expand Anthropic's brand presence in financial services, healthcare, and life sciences through media, content, and speaking opportunities
  • Provide communications counsel and media preparation for senior stakeholders, including Anthropic spokespeople and customer executives
  • Execute proactive media relations while effectively managing issues and reactive needs

You may be a good fit if you

  • Have 8-10 years of communications experience, ideally including in-house roles in AI or high-growth technology companies
  • Are genuinely excited about AI and care deeply about the safe development of transformative AI systems
  • Have a strong understanding of the European AI landscape—the companies, founders, investors, journalists, influencers, and policymakers shaping the conversation
  • Are experienced working with media in the UK, France and Germany
  • Are skilled at managing senior stakeholders and providing counsel to executives
  • Thrive in fast-paced environments and can balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution

Strong candidates may also have

  • Direct experience in communications for AI companies, high-growth startups, or research labs
  • Native or fluent proficiency in French and/or German
  • Existing relationships with key voices in European AI
  • Additional experience in financial services, healthcare, or life sciences communications
  • Understanding of AI policy dynamics in the EU and UK

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
