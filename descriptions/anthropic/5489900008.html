About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

Anthropic's Integrity & Compliance team helps the company meet its legal and regulatory obligations as our products, customers, and global footprint grow. We're looking for a Manager, Compliance & Regulatory Operations to lead the team that handles regulatory requests and complaints, copyright notices, and sanctions screening and operations. 

We're looking for an excellent operations manager. You'll manage a team and own how the work gets done: the queues, the systems, the operating model, and the outsourced partners who extend the team. The work is both high volume and high consequence. Some of these processes are established and need to scale. Others are still being built. Volumes are growing quickly across all of them, so you'll be running today's queues while designing the systems that will carry the next stage of growth. Claude is central to that plan, and you'll decide where it can take on the work and where people need to stay involved.

You'll work closely with our Regulatory Programs team as well as other I&C teams, who own the design and management of our regulatory and compliance programs. Your team runs the operations that put those programs into practice, working to the policy and standards those teams set. You'll also partner with Legal, Safeguards, Threat Intelligence, Engineering, and Support.

This is an operations leadership role, not a legal one. You need to understand how regulatory and compliance processes work, why these processes matter, and when a decision belongs to someone else.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead, coach, and develop a team across regulatory, copyright, and sanctions operations, and build cross-coverage so that no queue depends on a single person
  • Design and own the function's operating model, including how work comes in, who handles it, when it escalates, and what stays in-house or goes to outsourced partners
  • Manage queues across the team day to day, meeting SLAs and matching staffing to volume and priority
  • Own intake, case management, service levels, and escalation paths across the team, and lead decisions on our case management system
  • Lead the team's use of Claude, working with Engineering to automate triage and routine work, deciding which steps need human judgment, and checking the quality of what is automated
  • Manage outsourced providers day to day, including performance, service levels, procedures, and commercial terms
  • Set up a clear way to evaluate and accept new requests and projects, and make tradeoffs visible to stakeholders
  • Plan for sudden increases in volume, including surge coverage for complaint spikes and new product launches
  • Lead operational readiness for new products, business initiatives, and workflows, partnering with program owners, Legal, and Product so that requirements, controls, and capacity are in place before launch
  • Define and report the operational metrics that show leadership and auditors how the team is performing, such as volume, handling time, SLA adherence, quality, and backlog
  • Oversee operational support for recurring regulatory reporting and ad hoc data requests, ensuring program owners and Legal receive accurate, well-documented data on time
  • Run quality assurance on operational decisions against defined criteria, and turn the findings into training and process changes
  • Own and maintain operational procedures for each program, working to the standards program owners set and securing their approval of changes
  • Oversee regulatory operations, including triaging new requests, seeing them through to completion, and responding to complaints and inquiries across jurisdictions
  • Oversee copyright operations from intake through resolution, including takedown notices, opt-out requests, appeals, and repeat infringement tracking
  • Oversee sanctions screening operations, including referrals from partner teams, so that work meets SLAs and is escalated appropriately

Minimum qualifications

  • Significant people management experience in an operations environment, including setting priorities, coaching, and managing performance
  • Hands-on ownership of a high-volume compliance, regulatory, or similarly high-consequence operation with service levels, escalation paths, and quality checks
  • Track record of designing or significantly changing an operating model, including roles, handoffs, capacity, and what is kept in-house or outsourced
  • Experience managing outsourced vendors or managed service providers against performance and quality standards
  • Working knowledge of case management or workflow systems, including how to shape them around a team's process
  • Hands-on automation of operational work, including deciding what can be automated and checking the results
  • Procedure writing and recordkeeping that have held up to audit or regulatory review
  • Ability to analyze queue and case data and turn it into staffing and process decisions
  • Track record of taking a manual or inconsistent process and turning it into a repeatable, scalable, well-documented one
  • Strong cross-functional skills, including working with Legal, Compliance, and technical teams, influencing without direct authority
  • Sound judgment about which decisions you can make and which belong to Legal, policy owners, or leadership
  • Interest in AI and a commitment to Anthropic's mission of building safe, beneficial AI systems

Preferred qualifications

  • Sanctions operations experience, including restricted-party screening, alert disposition, and screening tools, ideally at a technology, payments, fintech, or financial services company
  • Familiarity with DMCA and other copyright notice processes
  • Exposure to online content or AI regulation, such as the EU Digital Services Act or EU AI Act
  • Background in privacy operations, such as data subject requests and user escalations
  • Experience handling regulator inquiries or consumer complaints across multiple jurisdictions
  • Experience supporting audits or regulatory reviews
  • Prior work selecting, implementing, or migrating a case management system
  • A track record of using Claude or other AI tools to automate triage or routine decisions, with quality checks that hold up to review
  • Comfort managing a team across time zones
  • Success stepping into an operations team during a reorganization or period of rapid growth

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
