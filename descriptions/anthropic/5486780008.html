About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About Beneficial Deployments

Beneficial Deployments ensures AI reaches and benefits the communities that need it most. We partner with nonprofits, foundations, and mission-driven organizations to deploy Claude in education, global health, economic mobility, and life sciences — focusing on raising the floor for those who need it most.

About the Role

We're looking for an Applied AI Architect to join Beneficial Deployments, focused on helping nonprofits and research institutes in APAC get real value from Claude. You'll be the technical lead for a portfolio of partners, working alongside our Account Executives from first conversation through deployment, and you'll support a wider set of accounts through learning sessions. With nonprofits, you'll find where Claude makes a real difference to their mission and their operations. With research institutes, you'll help researchers and the teams who support them use Claude in work they can check and stand behind. You'll design solutions that fit each partner's budget, data and teams, and turn early pilots into lasting adoption. What works for one partner becomes something others across the region and beyond can use.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as the primary technical advisor to a portfolio of nonprofits and research institutes from discovery through deployment, turning their priorities into scoped use cases with clear measures of success.
  • Partner with Account Executives on account plans, discovery and technical evaluation.
  • Design and demo solutions with Claude, the API and Claude Code and Science for nonprofits and research institutes
  • Help researchers evaluate Claude on their own questions and data before they rely on it, and be plain about its limits.
  • Guide partners through security review, data governance and procurement, including rules that differ by country and limits on how research data can be used.
  • Support a wider portfolio of accounts through learning sessions and enablement.
  • Run workshops and trainings that build AI fluency across partner teams, from program staff to research groups.
  • Build reusable assets (reference designs, connectors, prompt and eval templates) so one partner's work helps the next.
  • Spot patterns across partners, bring field insight back to Product and Research, and help partners measure what changed.
  • Travel up to 30% to partner sites for workshops, technical deep dives and relationship building.

You Might Be a Good Fit If You Have

  • 7-10 years in customer-facing technical roles such as solutions architecture, sales engineering, forward-deployed engineering or technical consulting.
  • A track record of getting lean, skeptical or slow-moving teams with many stakeholders to adopt technology and keep using it, including working through how they buy and govern it.
  • Hands-on experience building with LLM APIs: you have built and evaluated working prototypes or agents, and can guide a partner's own engineers on architecture and evals. You don't need to write production code.
  • The ability to earn the trust of researchers and other specialists: you can show how you tested something, where it fails and what you don't know yet.
  • Clear written and spoken communication across a broad audience. The ability to explain technical tradeoffs to program leads, researchers, operations staff and executives.
  • Patience with long buying and partnership cycles, a knack for knowing how to make them go smoothly, and real care for public benefit.
  • A track record of agency and autonomy: you spot what needs doing before anyone asks, agree that it matters, drive it to a finished result and know when to pull others in.
  • Comfort operating in ambiguity: you bring clarity to fuzzy problems, wear multiple hats and do whatever it takes to further the mission.
  • Bonus if you've worked with or at nonprofits, foundations, research institutes or other mission-driven organizations, worked in a sales team, built multilingual products, or worked with partners in more than one country.

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
