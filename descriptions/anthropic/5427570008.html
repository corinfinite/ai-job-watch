About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the Role

As the Head of GTM Strategy, Operations, and Analytics at Anthropic, you will lead our global GTM Strategy & Operations teams, as well as our GTM Analytics function, driving strategic decision-making and operational excellence across our rapidly scaling business. You'll manage growing teams responsible for GTM strategy, operations, and analytics, ensuring our sales, marketing, and customer success organizations have the insights, processes, and operational support needed to achieve ambitious growth targets while maintaining our commitment to safe AI deployment.

In this role, you'll be a key partner to GTM leadership, owning critical operating cadences, forecasting processes, and strategic planning initiatives. You'll drive data-driven decision making across the organization while building scalable systems and processes that support our mission to make AI safe and beneficial.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and scale teams across GTM Strategy & Operations and GTM Analytics, providing strategic direction and fostering a culture of operational excellence and analytical rigor
  • Own and optimize key GTM operating cadences, including forecasting processes, monthly and quarterly business reviews, and board-level reporting
  • Drive quarterly and annual planning processes for territory design, GTM headcount planning, quota setting, and target allocation
  • Establish central reporting frameworks and executive dashboards that provide actionable insights to GTM leaders and enable data-driven decision making
  • Partner closely with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success leadership to identify operational challenges and implement scalable solutions
  • Develop and maintain GTM performance metrics, KPIs, and reporting standards that align with company objectives and drive accountability
  • Lead cross-functional initiatives to optimize GTM efficiency, including process automation, system integrations, and workflow improvements
  • Provide strategic insights and recommendations to executive leadership on market opportunities, competitive positioning, and go-to-market effectiveness
  • Collaborate with Finance and Data Science teams to ensure alignment on revenue forecasting, pricing strategies, and market analysis
  • Partner closely with product organization to drive new product introductions, bring the voice of the customer and the field into roadmap and launch decisions, and shape pricing, packaging and sales readiness so each launch lands with the field prepared to sell it.
  • Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders across the organization, serving as a trusted advisor on GTM strategy and operations

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
