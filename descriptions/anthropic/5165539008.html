About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

We are seeking an experienced Tech Compensation Partner to join our Total Rewards team with a focus on competitive intelligence and market analysis. This role will drive strategic compensation decision-making through deep market research, data analysis, and competitive positioning for our technical talent across our Tech organizations. You'll be the go-to expert for understanding how Anthropic's compensation stacks up in the rapidly evolving AI talent market, while also serving as a program manager for our competitive intelligence initiatives for tech roles.

We're looking for someone who thrives as a strategic partner, is energized by data, and loves surfacing market insights. You're able to translate complex compensation trends into clear, actionable strategies. In this role, you'll partner closely with Recruiting and People Analytics to ensure our offers are both competitive and compelling, while building scalable programs that keep us ahead of a shifting market.

Responsibilities:

  • Serve as the dedicated compensation business partner for the Technology org.

  • Strategic partner for compensation support for hiring managers, people partners, and talent partners.

  • Operate as a business partner and data provider by building the relationships and context with tech leaders, People Partners, and recruiting that enable you to navigate ambiguous problems and recommend strategic solutions that make sense for our unique needs

  • Own comp cycle execution for your client groups end-to-end which include market benchmarking review, budget modeling, manager enablement, edge-case resolution, and exec-ready rollups in partnership with the broader Total Rewards team.

  • Lead competitive intelligence strategy for technical roles, conducting sophisticated market research and analysis to understand compensation trends, emerging pay practices, and talent movement patterns in AI, ML, and frontier technology companies

  • Utilizing comprehensive and innovative benchmarking methodologies to ensure we are gathering real-time market intelligence to stay ahead of market trends. This includes partnering closely with recruiting teams, hiring managers, and leaders to provide market insights, offer guidance, and competitive positioning recommendations for technical hires across all levels

  • Cross functional partnership to develop scalable processes, tools, and dashboards that enable self-service access to market data across the organization 

  • Build and maintain dynamic compensation models and frameworks specifically tailored to technical roles, incorporating factors like AI expertise, research impact, and specialized skills premiums

  • Create compelling data narratives and presentations for leadership, translating complex market analysis into clear strategic recommendations

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
