About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role:

As the leader of Applied AI for Startups across EMEA, you will lead Applied AI Architects and Applied AI Engineers to build technical mindshare around Anthropic’s products among early-stage adopters across EMEA: from London to Berlin, Paris to Tel Aviv, and beyond. You’ll work with the fastest-growing AI-focused startups in the region, driving adoption of frontier AI through bespoke LLM solutions. You’ll build and grow a cross-functional technical team, establish best practices for startup engagements, and represent Anthropic as a technical leader across the region.

In collaboration with Sales, Product, and Engineering, you’ll help startups incorporate AI into their products and platforms. A core part of this role is building Anthropic’s technical brand across the EMEA startup ecosystem through hands-on engagements, workshops, developer content, and strategic partnerships with accelerators and VCs.

Responsibilities:

  • Build, hire, lead, and mentor a team of Applied AI Architects and Applied AI Engineers supporting EMEA Startups
  • Partner with EMEA GTM leadership and Sales to build market-specific strategies that drive adoption and technical mindshare among startups
  • Engage hands-on with highest-value EMEA startups on advanced LLM integration, deployment, and adoption
  • Travel regularly across EMEA for customer workshops, ecosystem events, accelerator demo days, and conferences
  • Build technical mindshare among early-adopter startups through developer workshops, technical content, hackathons, and community programmes
  • Build relationships with VCs, accelerators, and incubators to position Anthropic as the platform of choice for emerging companies
  • Surface product feedback and market intelligence from EMEA startup engagements back to Product and Engineering

You may be a good fit if you have:

  • 7+ years of experience as a Forward Deployed Engineer, Customer Engineer, Sales Engineer, Applied AI Architect, or Applied AI Engineer
  • Minimum 4+ years of management experience (ideally 7+) leading hands-on, technical, customer-facing teams, with a track record of hiring and scaling teams rapidly
  • Experience building AI-native products and working with AI-native startups, ideally across multiple EMEA markets
  • Enjoy building distributed teams across geographies in unstructured environments
  • Strong executive presence and ability to build deep relationships across diverse cultural contexts
  • Familiarity with the architecture and operation of large language models
  • Passion for making powerful technology safe and societally beneficial
  • Willingness to travel frequently across EMEA (estimated 30–40% travel)

Nice to have:

  • Second-line management experience across different technical functions (e.g., managing both Applied AI Architecture and Engineering leads)
  • Founder or early-stage startup/scaleup experience: you understand the pace, constraints, and mindset of the companies you’ll be serving
  • Experience operating across multiple EMEA markets with understanding of regional startup ecosystems and go-to-market dynamics
  • Proficiency in one or more additional EMEA languages (e.g., French, German, Spanish, Italian, Swedish, Hebrew)

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
