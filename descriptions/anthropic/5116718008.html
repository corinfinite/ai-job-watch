About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About this role

This is not a traditional position. Claude's social presence is growing fast, and enterprise is where the next chapter of that growth lives. We're launching new owned surfaces for Claude for Enterprise and building the storytelling infrastructure that turns awareness on social into trust. This role owns that.

You'll sit at the intersection of brand, product, and sales — translating complex enterprise value into stories for enterprise buyers. You'll work closely with revenue marketing, solutions marketing, and our creative teams to tell the stories of enterprise customers. Reporting to the Organic Social Lead, you'll have significant autonomy to shape both strategy and execution.

Responsibilities

  • Build and launch Claude for Enterprise's social presence — define how Claude shows up for business buyers
  • Manage strategy, content calendar, voice, and day-to-day execution across relevant social channels
  • Develop customer stories and use-case content that enterprise buyers find useful and credible
  • Shape how product launches are communicated to an enterprise audience 
  • Coordinate with sales and demand-generation teams to ensure social content supports the broader enterprise pipeline
  • Track performance using engagement, sentiment, and other relevant metrics
  • Collaborate with the creative team on asset production

You may be a good fit if you have

  • 5+ years of experience in content marketing, social media, or enterprise comms
  • Strong editorial judgement and instincts for messaging and framing
  • Ability to operate with high autonomy in fast-moving, ambiguous environments — startup, agency, or founder experience strongly preferred
  • Experience building or scaling a LinkedIn presence for a B2B brand
  • Comfort working cross-functionally with sales, product, and creative teams
  • A genuine curiosity about AI and how organizations are adopting it
  • Willingness to become a deep Claude user

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
