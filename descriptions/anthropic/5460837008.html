About the role

We are seeking an International Finance Program Manager to join our International Accounting team in Dublin at Anthropic. In this role, you will drive execution excellence across a diverse portfolio of international expansion initiatives, standing up new go-to-market and infrastructure entities. You will partner closely with the Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Payroll and Finance Systems leads, functional business owners, and cross-functional teams to ensure initiatives are well-governed, on track, and delivering measurable business value.

This role requires someone who can context-switch across multiple finance domains, establish lightweight but effective program management practices, and thrive in a fast-paced environment where priorities evolve quickly. You will be instrumental in helping Anthropic scale its international footprint so that every new entity is ready to operate and meet its obligations from day one.

Responsibilities

  • Program Management: Manage a portfolio of international entity set-ups, ensuring clear prioritization, resource alignment, and delivery accountability across the Finance teams that own the deliverables

  • Governance & Process Design: Establish and maintain lightweight governance frameworks appropriate for a fast-paced, engineering-centric culture, including standardized project intake, status reporting, risk management, and decision escalation processes

  • Status Tracking & Reporting: Provide clear, consistent visibility into project health, milestones, dependencies, and risks through structured reporting to the Head of International Accounting, functional leads, and Finance leadership, and maintain the inventory of entities and the infrastructure expansion pipeline

  • Cross-Functional Coordination: Lead cross-functional coordination across Finance Operations, Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Payroll, Finance Systems, Legal and People Operations teams to drive alignment and remove blockers

  • Entity Set-up and Go-live Readiness: Track the finance set-up of each new entity (bank accounts, tax registrations, intercompany agreements, bank mandates, payroll, Workday and the accounting systems) and its go-live readiness, through the first payroll, first month-end close and first payroll tax or VAT filing, in support of the finance owner

  • Compliance Coordination: Partner with the Tax, Payroll and Accounting teams and local advisers to support the registrations, filings and compliance calendar of each new entity

  • Change Management: Drive user adoption for new entity processes and controls through structured change management, including training, communication plans, and post-go-live support

  • Vendor Management: Track and manage vendor delivery timelines, SOW commitments, and partner accountability across the banks and the payroll, tax and accounting providers in each country

  • AI Initiative Tracking: Coordinate AI automation initiatives within the international expansion portfolio, tracking progress on Claude-powered use cases and ensuring integration with broader Finance roadmap

Minimum Qualifications

  • Have 10+ years of experience in program management, project management, or PMO roles, with at least 3 years working in finance or accounting

  • Have hands-on experience managing complex entity set-ups, international expansion programs or multi-country roll-outs

  • Can manage multiple concurrent workstreams across different finance domains without losing sight of the details or the big picture

  • Are skilled at building trusted relationships with diverse stakeholders across Finance, Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Legal and People Operations teams

  • Have experience designing and implementing governance frameworks that provide accountability without excessive overhead, particularly in fast-paced, agile environments

  • Possess strong analytical skills and can synthesize complex project data into clear, executive-ready reporting

  • Are proficient with modern project management tools (Asana, Jira, or similar) and entity management tools and can quickly establish portfolio-level visibility and dashboards

  • Have excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex project information to both technical and non-technical audiences

  • Demonstrate strong problem-solving abilities and can navigate ambiguity while maintaining focus on delivery and deadlines

  • Are passionate about building scalable processes that support rapid organizational growth

Preferred Qualifications

  • Background in finance operations, accounting, or FP&A with understanding of month-end close, financial reporting, and compliance workflows

  • Experience with change management frameworks and methodologies

  • Experience working in high-growth technology companies

  • A professional accounting qualification (ACA, ACCA, CPA or equivalent)

  • A project management qualification

  • Interest in or experience with AI-powered automation and its application to finance processes

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
