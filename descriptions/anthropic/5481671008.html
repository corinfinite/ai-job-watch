About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

As GTM Narrative Lead at Anthropic, you will be the keeper of Anthropic's company story for customers. You will own the company narrative presentation end to end and keep it current as models and products ship, shaping how customers first hear who Anthropic is, what Claude changes for them, and why we are the right partner.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role in Solutions & Segment Marketing, within Enterprise Marketing. You will shape the storyline with senior leaders and write the words, the script, and the deck yourself. You will work closely with Product Marketing, Product, Research, Sales, Enablement, Brand, Design, and Communications to translate complex AI capabilities into compelling stories that resonate with enterprise buyers and reflect Anthropic's commitment to developing AI systems that are safe, steerable, and beneficial.

What you'll be shaping

Your narrative will be the starting point for customer conversations across executives, sellers, partners, and events. Each model and product launch changes what customers can do, and you will work directly with the people building it to turn it into a story a CEO can act on.

You will also set up how the work gets done: the customer-insight loop, the measures, and the Claude workflows that keep a story current at this pace. This role offers the opportunity to shape how one of the world's leading AI companies tells its story at a pivotal moment in the industry's development, while ensuring our narrative reflects our commitment to responsible AI deployment and societal benefit.

Key responsibilities

  • Own the company narrative: Own Anthropic's company narrative presentation from start to finish, including the storyline, the master deck, speaker notes, and the versions used by executives, sales, partners, and events

  • Translate capabilities into customer outcomes: Turn complex research and product capabilities into a clear story about what changes for customers, what it's worth, and why Anthropic is the right partner

  • Keep the story current through every launch: Lead the narrative update for every major launch, so the story is refreshed, approved, and in the hands of the field on launch day

  • Build and run a customer-insight loop: Use win/loss reviews, call recordings, executive briefings, customer advisory input, and usage data to keep testing and adjusting the storyline

  • Drive cross-functional alignment: Partner with Product Marketing, Brand, Design, and Communications to keep the company narrative aligned with positioning, messaging, and external voice

  • Measure whether the narrative works: Define and report on how widely the deck is used, how often key messages show up in deals, and feedback from customers and executives

  • Build workflows with Claude: Speed up research, customer feedback summaries, drafting, and versioning

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
