About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

As Anthropic's Data Center Security Operations Lead , you will own day-to-day physical security operations across our data center campuses in the area, including sites run with operating partners. You will be the person on the ground: the one who knows each site, its guard force, its local responders and what is actually happening on the floor.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role with no direct reports. You'll be on-site most weeks and get things done through people and companies you don't manage: site operators, guard vendors, construction and infrastructure partners, and your colleagues on the Data Center Security Engineering team. You will have the authority to make operational calls at your sites, and you'll work with the team's systems and delivery roles to take over each new site as it moves into operation.

The function is still in build mode. The work is ambiguous and changes as strategy changes. We need someone who can scope a loosely defined problem, carry it through to a result without waiting for a mature playbook, and treat security as an enabler of business outcomes.

Key responsibilities

  • Own the physical security operations program across our North East campuses, from a site's move into operations through steady state

  • Act as first call for site issues, including after hours, leading incident response and investigations and writing up what happened and what changes

  • Own guard force and vendor performance: post coverage, attrition, SLA grading, and the conversations that follow

  • Make time-sensitive operational and escalation decisions at your sites

  • Adapt company-wide standards into post orders, patrol standards, and badging and visitor procedures, keeping them consistent across sites and flagging where a standard doesn't fit

  • Partner with build, delivery and engineering teams on design and activation so that each turnover to operations is clean

  • Build and keep local relationships with law enforcement, emergency services, landlords and site operators

  • Lead cross-functional security work and represent your sites in infrastructure, compute and real estate decisions

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience running physical security operations across multiple sites in data centers, critical infrastructure or comparable high-security facilities

  • Experience managing a guard or integrator vendor against an SLA, using evidence and following through on missed performance

  • Experience leading physical security incident response, including after hours, and writing post-incident reports

  • Ability to turn standards into written site procedures and post orders that get adopted and followed

  • Ability to drive decisions with operators, vendors and internal partners without formal authority

  • Based near Buffalo or Rochester, or willing to relocate, and able to work on-site most weeks

  • Availability for after-hours on-call

  • Location: New York, based near Buffalo or Rochester

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience at a hyperscaler or major colocation operator

  • Experience taking a site from construction into live operations, including receiving the turnover

  • Experience in both owned data centers and colocation or partner-operated space, with an understanding of how the operator and tenant relationship changes controls, vendors and incident response

  • A track record of calibrating security to business reality, including explaining when you chose not to apply a control

  • A track record of re-scoping work without losing momentum when design or strategy shifts

  • CPP (Certified Protection Professional) or PSP (Physical Security Professional) certification

  • Experience building working relationships with law enforcement and emergency services, ideally in New York

  • Experience in a regulated environment, such as financial services or government

  • Experience supporting an external audit or customer security review of data center physical controls

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
