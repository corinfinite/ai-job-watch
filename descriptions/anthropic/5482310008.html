About the role

As Platform Docs Lead at Anthropic, you will be the single-threaded owner of developer documentation for the Claude Platform. You'll own the information architecture, the content, and the overall experience of platform.claude.com/docs — from a developer's first quickstart to the reference page they keep open in a pinned tab.

This role sits on the DevX team and is closer to DevRel than to traditional technical writing. The job is taste, judgment, and end-to-end developer empathy in addition to strong writing skills: you'll be expected to build with the Claude API yourself, understand the product firsthand, and turn that understanding into documentation developers genuinely love.

You'll also own documentation written for AI agents and contribute to the skill that teaches Claude how to help developers use the Claude API. Writing for an LLM reader is a new craft, and you'll be one of the people defining what good looks like.

Key responsibilities

  • Set and hold the quality bar for every page on platform.claude.com/docs — clarity, correctness, completeness, voice

  • Own the information architecture: structure the docs so developers and agents find what they need on the first try, and restructure when the product evolves underneath it

  • Write and rewrite the highest-leverage pages yourself; review and edit contributions from product, engineering, and DevRel partners

  • Own docs for new launches end-to-end, from early PRD through GA

  • Write docs for agents as well as humans

  • Ship code on the Claude API regularly so your understanding of the developer experience is firsthand, not secondhand

  • Contribute to the claude-api skill, and author and maintain related docs-for-agents artifacts, defining how documentation should be written for LLM consumers

  • Act as the primary customer of our internal docs-automation tooling, with a calibrated point of view on where Claude accelerates docs work and where human judgment is the bottleneck

  • Build feedback loops from support tickets, community channels, and product analytics into docs improvements, and measure whether those improvements actually reduce confusion

  • Partner closely with the DevX engineers who own the docs platform to shape the reading experience, navigation, search, and in-product docs surfaces

  • Translate recurring developer friction into concrete product and API feedback when documentation alone can't fix it

Minimum qualifications

  • You've been the end-to-end owner (not just a contributor) of a developer-facing surface like docs, SDKs, quickstarts, or a content program: setting a quality bar, holding it without being chased, and saying no to things that don't meet it

  • You're an excellent technical writer. You can take a confusing page and make it clear, take a correct page and make it useful, and explain why each change made it better

  • You have strong information architecture skills and can articulate why a docs site is structured well or badly within minutes of landing on it

  • You're hands-on technical. You write and read code in at least one programming language, you've shipped working software on top of APIs, and you can hold your own in a design discussion with engineers

Preferred qualifications

  • Background in developer relations, developer experience, developer education, or API product teams

  • Experience owning docs or developer content at an API-first or developer-tools company

  • Genuine curiosity about building with LLMs and a calibrated, non-hype view of what they're good at today

  • Experience writing content intended for LLM consumption (skills, system prompts, retrieval corpora)

  • Background in information architecture, UX writing, or content design

  • Experience working with docs-as-code toolchains and contributing to docs infrastructure

  • Public writing that demonstrates your voice (blog, newsletter, talks, open-source docs)

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
