About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the Role

You will design and lead a foundational talent pipeline that develops early-career professionals across Solutions Architecture, Pre-Sales AI Engineering, and Forward Deployed Engineering – as part of our Applied AI associate function. You’ll build a structured rotational program exposing associates to all major sales segments and Applied AI functions, culminating in capstone projects that produce scalable assets for the organization.

This is a high-impact, high-ownership role. You will design the program from the ground up – rotation structure, assessment frameworks, graduation criteria, onboarding curriculum, and capstone evaluation. You will personally coach early-career talent and work cross-functionally with Sales, Product, Engineering, and HR to produce world-class Applied AI professionals.

This role must be based in San Francisco, where all early enablement and training takes place.

Responsibilities

Program Design & Operations

  • Design and operate the end-to-end rotational program: onboarding, rotation scheduling, assessment checkpoints, capstone evaluation, and graduation decisions
  • Build rotation plans across Startups, Commercial, Digital Natives, and Industries segments

Talent Development & Coaching

  • Directly manage and coach Applied AI Associates through their rotational journey
  • Provide hands-on technical coaching on Claude Code, Agent SDK, MCP, eval design, and customer engagement

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Partner with segment leads to integrate associates into rotation teams
  • Coordinate with SA, PE, and FDE leadership for meaningful function exposure
  • Work with recruiting to build a diverse early-career technical talent pipeline
  • Partner with HR/Employment Law on program structure and performance management frameworks

Process & Operations

  • Build scalable processes for managing customer engagements, prioritizing opportunities, and measuring impact
  • Establish metrics and reporting frameworks to track team performance and customer outcomes
  • Create systems for knowledge management, ensuring best practices are documented and accessible
  • Drive operational excellence while maintaining the flexibility needed for a fast-growing product area

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
