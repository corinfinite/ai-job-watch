About the role

Anthropic is seeking an Accountant to join our Corporate Accounting team. You will be a hands-on member of the close: owning a defined set of accounts and entities from transaction through reconciliation, and making sure what lands in the financial statements is accurate, supported, and explainable.

This is a role for someone who likes getting into the detail and also asks why the process works the way it does. You will work closely with partners across Finance, Procurement, Payroll, Legal, Tax, and Finance Systems, and you will help us build a close that scales with the company, with a lot of room to improve how the work gets done as we grow.

Key responsibilities

  • Own month-end and quarter-end close activities for an assigned set of accounts and entities, including journal entries, accruals, account reconciliations, and supporting schedules, delivered on the close calendar

  • Prepare balance sheet reconciliations and flux analyses, investigate variances to root cause, and write clear explanations for reviewers and business partners

  • Maintain US GAAP supporting schedules and documentation for your areas of ownership, such as prepaids, fixed assets, accruals, and intercompany balances

  • Draft accounting memos and process documentation for routine and recurring transactions, and escalate non-routine items to Technical Accounting with a clear summary and a proposed treatment

  • Execute internal controls for your areas and prepare audit support, including schedules, evidence, and responses to auditor requests

  • Partner with business owners and finance colleagues to understand new transactions, resolve issues at the source, and reduce adjustments after close

  • Review work from contractors and team members, and give feedback that helps the team raise its quality bar

  • Identify and implement process improvements and automation, including using Claude and other AI tools, and check the output carefully before anyone relies on it

  • Support system and process changes in the accounting function, including testing, data validation, and documentation

Minimum qualifications

  • Working knowledge of US GAAP, applied hands-on to the monthly close: journal entries, accruals, reconciliations, and financial statement support

  • Experience preparing account reconciliations and flux analyses in an ERP or general ledger system (for example, Workday, NetSuite, or equivalent)

  • Strong spreadsheet skills, including lookups, pivot tables, and reconciling large data sets in Excel or Google Sheets

  • Experience supporting external audits and operating internal controls

  • Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to explain accounting results to colleagues who are not accountants

  • Sound judgment on when to resolve an issue independently and when to escalate it

  • Attention to detail and a strong sense of ownership, with the ability to manage competing deadlines

Preferred qualifications

  • 7+ years of progressive accounting experience, in public accounting, in industry, or a combination of both

  • CPA, ACA, ACCA, or CA qualification, or progress toward one

  • Experience in a fast-moving environment where processes were still being defined, with a track record of improving them

  • Familiarity with technical areas such as ASC 842, ASC 718, ASC 606, fixed assets, or multi-entity and intercompany accounting

  • Experience with close management or reconciliation tooling, or with SQL or Python for data analysis

  • Experience with an ERP migration, SOX readiness, or public company reporting

  • A track record of using AI tools to speed up accounting or finance work, with checks that hold up to review

  • Genuine interest in AI and Anthropic's mission to build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
