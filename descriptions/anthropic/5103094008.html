About the Role: 

We are seeking an experienced IP Legal Specialist to join our growing legal team and serve as the operational backbone of our intellectual property program. In this role, you will partner closely with our IP attorneys and outside counsel to help manage our trademark, domain, and patent portfolios, lead our online brand protection efforts, and ensure flawless execution of our IP protection strategies. This is an excellent opportunity for a seasoned IP professional who thrives on organization, wants to make a significant impact at a cutting-edge AI company, and is aligned with Anthropic’s mission. San Francisco Bay Area strongly preferred.

Responsibilities: 

  • Lead Anthropic's online brand protection program, managing relationships with external vendors, reviewing, actioning, and tracking a heavy volume of infringements, and escalating enforcement matters to IP attorneys and outside counsel where appropriate
  • Manage global trademark portfolio administration, including application filings, office action response coordination, and portfolio maintenance across multiple jurisdictions, in coordination with IP attorneys and outside counsel
  • Support patent administration, including processing invention disclosures, coordinating patent application filings, gathering inventor signatures on assignments and other patent prosecution documents, managing annuity payments, and maintaining patent records
  • Maintain and manage IP docketing systems, ensuring all IP assets are properly tracked and monitored
  • Conduct preliminary trademark clearance searches to support new product launches, features, and brand initiatives
  • Manage domain name portfolio, including new registrations, renewals, and transfers, and other domain-related initiatives
  • Organize and maintain IP documentation and records to support due diligence activities and strategic initiatives

You may be a good fit if you have:

  • 10+ years of IP operations specialist experience with demonstrated deep expertise in both trademark and patent portfolio management
  • Strong proficiency with IP management software systems (Symphony or similar platforms)
  • Substantial experience with brand protection programs and enforcement activities, including online infringement monitoring and takedown coordination
  • Proven track record of managing complex, multi-jurisdictional IP portfolios
  • Extensive experience coordinating with and managing relationships with outside counsel firms and external vendors domestically and internationally
  • Excellent organizational skills, meticulous attention to detail, and ability to juggle multiple complex projects simultaneously
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively with attorneys, business teams, and external vendors at all levels
  • Self-starter mentality with ability to work independently and proactively identify and solve problems
  • A sense of humor

Strong candidates may have:

  • Deep knowledge of international filing procedures (Madrid Protocol, PCT, Paris Convention)
  • Experience in AI, machine learning, or related cutting-edge technologies 
  • Background in high-growth technology companies or startups 
  • Experience with contract management and legal operations tools
  • Project management experience or certification

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
