About Anthropic

Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced securities lawyer to join the Corporate Legal team as Corporate Counsel, Public Company Reporting & Governance. In this role, you will prepare our SEC filings, advise on securities law and corporate governance, and support the Board and its committees. You will also partner closely with finance, accounting, investor relations, and equity teams to build the systems, controls, and repeatable processes that accurate, timely disclosure depends on. 

This is a hands-on role with meaningful scope and visibility. You will provide strategic, practical advice to senior leaders and teams across the company. You will draft and review disclosure yourself, while also helping build the processes and workflows that allow the function to scale.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare and review SEC filings, including Forms 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K, the proxy statement and Section 16 filings
  • Advise on compliance with SEC and Nasdaq rules
  • Help establish and run our disclosure controls and procedures, including the disclosure committee and the reporting calendar
  • Support our securities compliance policies and the administration of the Insider Trading Policy, including trading windows, pre-clearance and 10b5-1 plans
  • Review earnings materials, press releases, investor presentations and other significant external and internal communications
  • Partner with Investor Relations on Regulation FD compliance, stockholder engagement and the annual meeting
  • Prepare Board and committee materials, draft minutes and resolutions, and maintain governance policies and committee charters
  • Support executive and equity compensation disclosure with the People and Equity teams
  • Advise the teams that report publicly on our mission and safety work
  • Keep partner teams informed of governance trends and SEC and Nasdaq developments
  • Contribute to the processes and tools that let a small team handle public company work at scale

Minimum Qualifications

  • Hold a law degree (JD, LLB, or equivalent) and meet the local licensing requirements to work as in-house counsel where you’ll be based (California or New York preferred)
  • Significant public company reporting experience. This role is focused on periodic reporting and governance, and is not a capital markets or M&A role
  • Deep knowledge of corporate and securities law, including SEC and Nasdaq rules
  • Experience giving accurate, practical advice in real time at a fast-growing company
  • A working understanding of accounting and corporate finance
  • A track record of building strong working relationships across teams
  • Clear written and verbal communication with executives and non-lawyers, without legal jargon

Preferred Qualifications

  • At least 8 years of relevant legal experience, including securities and corporate governance experience at a law firm and in-house at a public company
  • Experience building processes and procedures to public-company standards
  • Experience supporting a public company board and its committees
  • A track record of standing up a process or program 
  • The ability to translate securities law requirements into clear, practical guidance for business teams
  • Sound judgment and the ability to advise with incomplete information

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
