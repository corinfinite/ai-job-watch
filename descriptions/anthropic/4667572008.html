About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the Role

Enterprise is central to Anthropic's mission. The organizations that could benefit most from Claude in healthcare, financial services, and government are often the most demanding buyers, with rigorous requirements around security, compliance, and control. We believe earning their trust is essential to ensuring AI benefits the world broadly.

We're looking for a software engineer to join our Enterprise Foundations team—the team that makes Claude enterprise-ready at scale. When a Fortune 500 company wants to roll out Claude to 100,000 employees, we're the team that makes it possible.

You'll build the foundational systems that large organizations require before they can deploy AI at scale: user and permissions management, security and compliance features, admin experiences, and analytics for organizations. This work directly converts product-market fit into revenue by removing the deployment blockers that prevent large organizations from adopting Claude broadly.

This role sits at the intersection of enterprise product, platform infrastructure, and go-to-market. You'll partner closely with product, design, sales, and customer success to understand what our largest customers need, then translate those requirements into scalable technical solutions that work across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and API.

Core Responsibilities

  • Design and build permission infrastructure including role-based access control, role management
  • Develop admin experiences that make complex access control intuitive—enabling admins to beta test features with pilot groups, set spend controls by department, and manage permissions at scale
  • Build enterprise analytics capabilities that give admins a quantitative and qualitative view of how Claude is used in their organization in a privacy-preserving way
  • Design and implement core security needs such as customer-managed keys and tenant isolation
  • Design, build, and ship enterprise-grade products with high standards for security, reliability, and scalability
  • Collaborate closely with Product Platform, Enterprise End User, GTM, and Verticals teams to ensure architecture works across all Anthropic products

You May Be a Good Fit If You Have

  • 5+ years of experience as a software engineer, with experience building enterprise SaaS platforms or admin tools
  • Experience spanning both product and platform teams - working directly with customers while designing scalable and stable systems
  • Ability to work effectively across product, design, sales, and customer success functions
  • Ability to move quickly, push through ambiguity, consolidate multiple points of view, and align stakeholders across engineering, product, design, and GTM
  • Motivation to work in a fast-moving environment where enterprise requirements evolve rapidly

Strong Candidates May Also Have

  • Experience with AI/ML products and understanding how enterprises evaluate and deploy AI tools
  • Background with compliance frameworks for regulated industries (SOC2, HIPAA) and enterprise audit logging requirements
  • Experience building permissions, billing, or pricing infrastructure
  • Familiarity with data residency and sovereignty requirements across global regions
  • Startup experience, particularly in scaling enterprise platforms from early adoption to broad deployment

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
