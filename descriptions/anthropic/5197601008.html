About the role

As Programme Manager, Partner Delivery on Anthropic's Global Partnerships & Business Development team, you'll build and run a partner-delivered package programme from scratch. You'll turn customer needs into productised offerings that partners sell and deliver — starting with Activations — and own the programme end-to-end, from partner recruitment through delivery outcomes.

This is a zero-to-one role. You'll establish the infrastructure, the economics, and the operational playbook that enables Anthropic's partners to consistently deliver high-quality outcomes at scale.

Key responsibilities

  • Design and launch a productised package programme: fixed scope, fixed price, defined outcomes — built for repeatability
  • Own the partner-matching engine: translate customer needs into the right package, route to the right partner through CRM with clear SLAs and fallback logic
  • Manage inbound sales requests end-to-end: triage, match, brief the partner, loop in internal account teams, and track delivery to completion
  • Recruit and certify a delivery partner bench across tiers and regions
  • Define the unit economics: pricing, margin structure, investment thresholds, and revenue attribution
  • Build and maintain both the partner-facing delivery kit and internal playbook, including intake routing, talk-tracks, and escalation paths
  • Instrument each package against its promised outcomes; double down on what works and cut what doesn't

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience running a partner-delivered services or packaged-offering programme at a hyperscaler, SaaS company, or systems integrator
  • Demonstrated ability to productise professional services — turning bespoke consulting into repeatable offerings partners can sell and deliver without custom scoping each time
  • Experience building or restructuring partner-matching and routing workflows in a CRM
  • Experience owning the sales-to-partner handoff and accountability for customer outcomes
  • Solid grounding in partner economics: co-investment models, margin structures, and shared revenue attribution

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience working across product marketing, sales, partner, and customer success functions without direct ownership of any single one
  • Familiarity with Salesforce or equivalent CRM for routing and SLA tracking
  • Track record of building programmes in ambiguous, early-stage environments where the structure didn't exist yet

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
