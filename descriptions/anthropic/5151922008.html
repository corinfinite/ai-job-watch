About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

Anthropic is expanding rapidly across international markets. As International Expansion Lead, you will be the tip of the spear — designing market entry strategy, building landing Sales teams, and driving early commercial traction in priority markets across APAC, EMEA, and LATAM. You'll incubate each new market until a permanent leader is in place: owning the GTM strategy, supporting lighthouse deals, and creating the conditions for a full sales team to scale. When you're not in-market, you're running executive engagement programs that make Anthropic leaders more impactful globally and international customers feel the full weight of the company behind them.

Responsibilities

  • Develop the global expansion roadmap with clear, compelling business cases to justify each new market expansion decision 
  • Design and execute market entry strategy for priority international markets, directing complex workstreams across a wide range of cross-functional teams, working from zero to initial commercial traction
  • Act as interim lead for new markets: recruit local landing teams, win early lighthouse deals, and establish operational foundations
  • Run executive engagement programs — preparing Anthropic leadership for international market visits and orchestrating inbound customer executive visits to HQ for maximum commercial impact
  • Partner with Marketing, Communications, and Global Affairs to adapt Anthropic's narrative and GTM approach to local market contexts
  • Identify and develop strategic partnerships that accelerate market entry where direct presence alone is insufficient
  • Serve as the primary bridge between international Sales teams and HQ, translating on-the-ground signals into priorities for product, policy, and leadership
  • Build playbooks and repeatable processes that allow Anthropic to enter new markets faster over time
  • Serve as the GTM lead for Anthropic’s mission-oriented international programs related to expansion — including expanding access and affordability in Global South markets

You might be a good fit if you have

  • 5–7 years in GTM, business development, or market expansion roles — with clear evidence of operating internationally, not just advising on it
  • Track record of building something from zero in a new geography: first deals, first hires, first partnerships
  • Comfortable operating at both strategic and transactional altitude — you can write the market entry thesis and also run the deal process
  • Strong executive presence and communication skills; able to represent Anthropic credibly to government officials, enterprise leaders, and global partners
  • High tolerance for ambiguity; you define the role as much as you execute it
  • Willingness to travel frequently across APAC, EMEA, and LATAM

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
