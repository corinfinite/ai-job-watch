About the role

We are looking for a detail-oriented Real Estate Construction Project Manager to join our Workplace team at Anthropic. This critical role will be a key contributor for our global real estate projects across multiple office locations, serving as the central liaison between external project managers, vendors, and internal stakeholders. The ideal candidate will bring 8+ years of corporate real estate construction project management experience, with exceptional organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple concurrent projects, maintain meticulous attention to detail in a fast paced environment.  This role will also support developing project management office (PMO) functions and tools with guidance from the Head of Construction. 

Key responsibilities

Project Management (70%)

  • Own the delivery of multiple small-mid size real estate projects up to 50,000 square feet across an international portfolio, ensuring swift project set up, timely completion, and within budget parameters
  • Serve as primary liaison between external project managers and internal stakeholders, facilitating clear communication and project alignment
  • Represent Anthropic’s interests while managing vendors including contract and invoice reviews
  • Accountable for project milestones, deliverables, financials, and potential risks across all active projects
  • Oversee project meetings and ensure follow-through on all commitments

Project Management Office (PMO) Development (30%)

  • Partner with the Head of Construction to develop internal PMO tools including templates for vendor RFPs, bid leveling, project charters, project kick off agendas, and lessons learned protocols
  • Build and maintain project reporting dashboards, cost benchmarks, and KPI tracking that give leadership real visibility into portfolio health
  • Establish project delivery playbooks and project control checklists for recurring project types (coworking activations, fit-outs, restacks) to ensure efficient and high quality project delivery   
  • Develop project documentation standards to ensure files are properly maintained and accessible 
  • Launch and manage new PMO tools and standards on live projects, review outcomes, and collect feedback for continuous improvement

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience in corporate real estate construction project management on the client-occupier side 
  • Demonstrated experience managing external project managers, general contractors, and FF&E vendors; comfortable reviewing contracts and invoices
  • Outstanding verbal and written communication abilities to ensure regular and clear project team and stakeholder communication
  • Experience with developing and implementing PMO tools and processes seamlessly with in-flight projects  
  • Proficient with Google Suite applications
  • Work independently while knowing when to escalate issues appropriately

Preferred qualifications

  • Have minimum 8 years of relevant experience
  • Have international real estate project management experience in rapid-growth technology company 
  • Demonstrate experience with process improvement and operational excellence
  • PMP certification or equivalent

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
