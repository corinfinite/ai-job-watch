About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

About the role

Claude Academy is where millions of people learn to use Claude and build fluency with AI. Our team built and launched it in-house. Now we're working out what learning looks like when AI is not just the subject of the lesson, but how we deliver learning.

We're a small team of engineers, technical writers and designers, and we work closely with the educators who create the courses. We're hiring a product-minded software engineer to own large parts of Academy end-to-end, from the lesson a learner opens to the tools that get it published.

Most of the hard problems here are ambiguous. You'll decide what's worth building, bring other teams along, and see it through to something people use. You can read more about how we approach teaching and learning AI.

Responsibilities

  • Own projects end to end: scope with stakeholders, design, build, ship, measure and operate, including security and privacy review
  • Decide what to build by talking to learners, admins and experts, instrumenting what you ship, and stopping what doesn't work
  • Build across the stack, from React and TypeScript front ends to the Go services behind them
  • Build learning experiences powered by Claude, along with the evals that show whether they served the learner
  • Drive work that crosses team lines, with product, security, privacy, infrastructure and go-to-market partners
  • Raise the quality of the team's work through design docs, code review and mentoring

Where you might focus

Everyone works across Academy. Depending on your strengths, you'll lead in one of two areas:

  • Learner and enterprise product. The experience of learning on Academy, and what companies need to bring to their teams: hands-on practice, assessment, credentials, reporting and integrations.
  • Content systems. How content gets made and stays correct as fast as the product changes: authoring, preview, publishing and rollback for people who aren't engineers, Claude-assisted drafting with human review, reviewed versions for an industry or role, and checks that catch what's out of date.

You may be a good fit if you

  • Have 7+ years of software engineering experience, including products you owned from scoping through iteration in production
  • Are strong across the stack, and can build both a polished front-end and the production services behind it
  • Know the people you build for, whether they're learners, admins or authors, and can point to something you changed because of what you learned from them
  • Have led technically complex, ambiguous work across teams, and can explain the architecture and its tradeoffs in depth
  • Make the engineers around you better through review, design and example
  • Have shipped LLM-powered features, or systems that depend on generated output, and can show how you knew the output was good
  • Finish things, including the unglamorous last part

Strong candidates may also have

  • Built learning tools, taught, or written tutorials, courses or developer education
  • Built authoring tools, a CMS, a docs platform or a publishing pipeline that non-engineers rely on
  • Run experiments on activation, retention or learning outcomes, and can say what moved and why
  • Built evals or automated quality checks for generated content
  • Worked on assessment, credentials or enterprise administration features
  • Worked on localization, structured content (such as MDX) or server rendering for content-heavy sites
  • A strong design sense, and the habit of sweating the details of a user-facing surface

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
