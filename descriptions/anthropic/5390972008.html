About the role

Anthropic's Go-To-Market team is growing quickly, and every new Account Executive, BDR, and GTM teammate we hire needs to become productive, confident, and credible on Claude faster than at any other company. The GTM Onboarding program is one way that happens. It's the first experience new hires have of how we sell, who our customers are, what our products do, and how we work together — and it sets the trajectory for their ramp.

We're looking for someone to own that program end to end. You'll design and run recurring onboarding cohorts for new GTM hires: building the curriculum, coordinating facilitators from across Sales, Marketing, Applied AI, and Product, running live sessions, and making sure every cohort leaves ready to have real customer conversations. This person will be the front door to GTM. You will set the tone for new hires’ first impression of Anthropic and our team.

You'll also own what happens after onboarding ends. That means measuring ramp, tracking attendance and comprehension, gathering feedback, and using what you learn to improve the next cycle. You'll build and maintain the operational machinery behind the program — rosters, calendars, facilitator communications, mock discovery calls — and you'll use Claude itself to automate as much of it as possible.

This is a highly cross-functional role. You'll partner with GTM leadership, hiring managers, Revenue Operations, Marketing, and product teams to keep the program current as our products and sales motions evolve. You’ll work closely with subject matter experts, but you'll need to be a generalist who can help new hires connect the dots and build robust mental models for how the business operates. 

This role reports into the GTM Enablement team.

Key responsibilities

  • Own the GTM Onboarding program end to end: curriculum design, cohort scheduling, facilitator coordination, live delivery, and continuous improvement
  • Design and deliver training that makes Anthropic's products, sales motions, customer stories, and tools (Salesforce, Gong, Apollo, and others) accessible to sellers from a range of backgrounds
  • Serve as an extension to subject matter experts on Claude for Enterprise, Claude Code, and the Claude Developer Platform for new GTM hires, and keep onboarding content accurate as products change
  • Recruit, prepare, and support a rotating bench of internal facilitators and field volunteers, including briefing them on each cohort's composition and running mock discovery calls with new AEs
  • Build and maintain the program's operational infrastructure — rosters, calendar logistics, and communications — and automate recurring work using Claude
  • Measure program effectiveness through attendance, comprehension checks, feedback surveys, and ramp metrics, and present findings and recommendations to GTM leadership
  • Build and maintain 30/60/90 day ramp plans and post-onboarding resources that carry new hires from cohort to quota
  • Partner with hiring managers, Revenue Operations, Marketing, and product teams to identify enablement gaps and incorporate new content into the program
  • Work with the global enablement team to adapt and extend the program for international GTM teams and new role types as the organization grows

Minimum qualifications

  • Experience in sales enablement, and/or program management-related roles
  • Have designed and delivered onboarding or training programs for sales teams, including building curriculum and facilitating live sessions
  • Can translate technical product concepts into clear, practical language for non-technical audiences
  • Bring strong program management skills, with a track record of running recurring, multi-stakeholder operations reliably
  • Apply adult learning principles to how you design and sequence training
  • Communicate clearly and credibly with new hires, facilitators, and senior GTM leadership
  • Work effectively in fast-moving environments with high ambiguity, and are comfortable building where no process exists yet
  • Are excited about Anthropic's mission to develop AI that is safe and beneficial

Preferred qualifications

  • 6+ years experience in sales enablement, and/or program management-related roles
  • Experience running sales onboarding at a company that scaled significantly
  • Have used Claude or similar AI tools to automate operational workflows, and are enthusiastic about doing more of it
  • Experience measuring and reporting on sales ramp performance
  • Experience adapting enablement content for international or multi-language audiences
  • Prior experience as a quota-carrying seller, sales engineer, or customer-facing technical role

How we're different

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
